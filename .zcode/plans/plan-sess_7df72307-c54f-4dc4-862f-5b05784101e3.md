## Lever 1 — reasoning effort (the one-line root-cause fix)

Replace `AUTHORING_BUDGET` (`:391`):

```ts

// AFTER

```

Rewrite the doc-comment above it to record *why*: `max_tokens` is a ceiling that OpenAI models effectively ignore; `effort` is the quality axis. This is the biggest single quality lever. GPT-5.6-terra authoring moves from provider-default (medium) to high effort.

## Lever 3 — resolve the prompt contradiction (pure prompt rewrite)

The current authoring prompts contradict themselves:
- `AUTHOR_SYSTEM` + `DRAFT_INTENT`: "write it PROPERLY, your craft, idiomatic, correct at the edges"
- `OVERWRITE_SCOPE`: "every other region byte-for-byte identical. Do not reformat, reorder, rename, 'improve', or tidy anything the task did not name."

A model told both at once conservatively picks the second and ships a rigid diff. The fix is to **partition** the file into two zones rather than list conflicting verbs:

- **CHANGE ZONE** — the regions the task and draft name. Full craft: restructure, rename, fix an adjacent bug you can see, modernize. The strong model is free to do this well.
- **KEEP ZONE** — everything else in the file. Byte-identity: reproduce unchanged, do not delete, do not reformat.

This is a single coherent rule ("do excellent work inside the change zone; preserve everything outside it byte-for-byte") instead of two warring instructions. It applies to **all ratings** — no plumbing flag, no `highLatitude` field, no gate. Medium gets it too (tight where it should be: keep-zone is strict), so I'm dropping the earlier "high-only" over-engineering entirely. The `edit` (anchored region) path keeps its existing seam discipline — latitude lives in the full-file `write` path where it belongs.

Rewrites:
1. **`OVERWRITE_SCOPE`** — replace the "do not reformat/rename/improve" prohibitions with the change-zone/keep-zone partition. Keep the load-bearing phrase `anything you omit is DELETED` (asserted by `draft-intent.test.mjs:160`).
2. **`DRAFT_INTENT`** — keep its core ("bounds scope only, not quality"; "DO NOT COPY"; "write it PROPERLY"; "defeats the entire point of escalating to you") because those are all asserted (`draft-intent.test.mjs:96-117`), but reconcile it with the new zone framing: the draft defines the *change zone*, not the quality.
3. **`AUTHOR_SYSTEM`** — tighten the "fulfill the task exactly / idiomatic / correct at the edges" clause so it no longer reads as "stay inside the sketch."

All asserted substrings are preserved: `/BOUNDS SCOPE ONLY — NOT QUALITY/`, `/TAKE FROM IT: which region to change/`, `/Do not widen the change beyond what it touches/`, `/DO NOT COPY: its structure, naming, patterns, or level of quality/`, `/write it PROPERLY instead/`, `/defeats the entire point of escalating to you/`, `/anything you omit is DELETED/i`.

**Test impact:** none — I will keep every asserted phrase verbatim.
